How to read the labels

Two label systems, both kept

Labels say what kind of statement something is. They are not quality scores.

Research library labels

From the imported research method.

Verified
Checked against a primary or official source during the research pass.
Interpretation
A reasoned framing or architectural reading, not a sourced fact.
Speculative
Worth exploring, not established.
Verify
A useful lead whose licence, maintenance or applicability still needs checking.

Sponge catalogue and Data Charter classes

From the sponge catalogue and the Data Charter.

Observed
Published by an authority, quoted as published.
Derived
Computed by the team from open Basel data, method named, for explaining and screening only.
Assumed
The team's reasoning or proposal; not measured.
Unknown
No open data found in the reviewed Basel and federal sources.

Basel's own sponge-city example

Basel-Stadt coordinated greening and unsealing with district-heating works. The library notes this makes planned works a potentially important evidence layer: not only where need is high, but where action is unusually feasible now.

From Data & sources · VERIFIED

Modelling caution

Do not invent one opaque sponge score by adding incomparable quantities. Heat, runoff, shade, biodiversity, cost and implementation complexity should stay separate unless a documented objective function is chosen.
