The data question

Unknown to us is not unknown to the city

The team's gatekeeper model asks who holds the evidence, what unlocks it, and which decision waits until then. Role names come from the team's research and still need confirmation with the authorities.

Decision-relevant factAccessGatekeeperDecision blocked until then
Utility conflicts under a footprintRestricted · operator-heldCadastral authority and the relevant network operatorCan this footprint proceed to detailed design?
Drainage destination and sewer contextRestrictedTiefbauamt drainage and general drainage plan (GEP) owners; property records for private drainageWhere can retained or overflow water safely go?
Infiltration feasibilityMixedEnvironmental authorities for context; a qualified site investigation for the local valueIs infiltration credible at this footprint?
Performance after constructionProject-heldProject owner, research partner or monitoring operatorDoes a built design perform as expected?
