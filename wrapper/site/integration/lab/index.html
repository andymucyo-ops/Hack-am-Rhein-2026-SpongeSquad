Change one street. Follow the water.

One synthetic street, one rain garden and an explicit water network. The three stages below use the same running lab, so switching stages keeps your changes.

Question

What can change, and which measures depend on one another?

Works now

  • Add a rain garden in place of the north parking strip
  • Connect street runoff through a kerb opening; this step needs the garden first
  • Reset restores the exact baseline

Illustrative

  • The 60 × 30 m street is a schematic, not a surveyed Basel street
  • 12 m³ storage, the infiltration rate and three removed parking spaces are assumptions

Still missing

  • Real site geometry, ownership and feasibility
  • A second intervention and sourced effect ranges

Question

How might rainwater move differently after the change?

Works now

  • Minute-by-minute water balance for a chosen storm depth
  • Same-minute comparison against the baseline
  • Eleven model tests, including conservation of water at every frame

Illustrative

  • Default storm of 30 mm in 30 minutes: 54 m³ to the sewer when sealed, 40 m³ with a connected garden
  • No calibration, evaporation, travel delay or flooding; the sewer is an unlimited sink

Still missing

  • Calibrated hydrology and Basel design storms
  • Sewer capacity and any heat effect

Question

Illustrative

  • The diagram is schematic
  • The explainer's water shares and heat indicator are scenario values

Still missing

  • Bala's explainers and Mary's story are not embedded yet
  • A view of a real site
