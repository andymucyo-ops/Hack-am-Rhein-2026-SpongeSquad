Where should we look first?

Andy's site-scoping tool screens Basel for places where heat and water problems may overlap. It is a discussion tool for choosing where to investigate, not a hazard map.

Question

Where in Basel might a heat-and-water intervention be worth investigating?

Works now

  • Leaflet map of Basel with 4 discussion areas and 16 exploration pins
  • Explainable provisional score, shortlist and side-by-side comparison
  • Dataset catalogue with Basel-Stadt and opendata.swiss connectors

Illustrative

  • Every candidate location and indicator value is a placeholder
  • The ranking must not be presented as a factual claim

Still missing

  • Spatial join of official heat, land-cover, canopy and runoff layers
  • Basel-Stadt boundary checks and field validation
  • An export of the map selection to the next step
