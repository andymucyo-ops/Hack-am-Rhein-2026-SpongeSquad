01 Intro / problem 00:45

When rain leaves too fast, the city keeps the heat.

Sealed streets send water away just when the city needs it most. A sponge city slows rain down, stores it and makes room for it to return.

Basel is already acting on climate adaptation. Our challenge was not to invent the idea, but to make a complex system easier to understand, explore and discuss at street level.
