Signals exist

Climate, canopy, land-cover and hazard information answer different questions in different places.

Derived

Screening is not design

A city-scale signal can identify a place to inspect. It cannot provide street geometry or engineering evidence.

Unknown

Deciding facts are missing

Utilities, drainage, soil, ownership and site conditions remain explicit evidence gates.
